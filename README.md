# **Brewview**
___

## What is Brewview?
Brewview is a full-stack web application designed to help users discover, journal, and share their beverage experiences.
Users can log in, add photos, descriptions, and ratings for drinks they’ve tried, and track them by location.
The goal is to create a personalized journey through beverages helping users remember what they’ve enjoyed, explore new favorites, and make every drinking experience more intentional and enjoyable.

## Problem Statement
<u>Context</u>:
Exploring new beers, ciders, and beverages is an experience worth remembering, yet most people have no consistent way to keep track of what they’ve tried, where they had it, or how it made them feel. Notes get scattered across phones, photos, or social media, and over time, those unique tasting moments fade away. There’s no simple, organized way to record, rate, and revisit those personal beverage experiences all in one place.

<u>Core Problem</u>:
There is no simple, unified platform for users to log and review their beverage experiences while tracking where they’ve been and what they’ve enjoyed. Existing tools like notes apps or review platforms are often too general or lack personalization, making it difficult for users to build a meaningful record of their journey.

<u>Impact on Users</u>:
Many enthusiasts struggle to remember what they tried at different locations, how it tasted, or whether they’d want it again.
This leads to repetitive choices, missed opportunities to discover new favorites, and a lack of personal insight into their preferences.

<u>Opportunity for Solution</u>:
By building Brewview, a full-stack web application that allows authenticated users to add, rate, and describe beverages by location, users can create a personalized “beverage journal.”
Each entry includes a name, photo, description, rating, and location; giving users a simple and intuitive way to document their tasting experiences.

The platform tailors each user’s experience based on their recorded preferences and history, gradually becoming a reflection of their unique taste journey.
Over time, Brewview empowers users to revisit their favorite drinks, discover new brews intentionally, and learn from their evolving palate.

Through intuitive journaling, smart caching, and personalized insights, Brewview helps users capture, rate, and relive every tasting experience ensuring they never forget a great beer again.
